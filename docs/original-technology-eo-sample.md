# Original Technology E&O Portfolio Questionnaire

This independent sample is a portfolio exercise for technology errors and omissions intake. Its wording and example business were created for this project. It is not an insurer application, quote, eligibility decision, or substitute for a current carrier form.

## Fictional business: Northstar Systems Studio LLC

Northstar builds internal workflow software for small businesses, implements client software, and provides training. Its sample intake mentions client system access and occasional subcontractors. All names, figures, and answers below are invented.

## Sample intake questions

1. What is the business's legal name and website?
2. Explain its products and services in plain language.
3. What is its expected annual revenue this year?
4. How is revenue divided among consulting, implementation, software development, training, and resale? Do the shares add to 100%?
5. Does it host software, process online transactions, hold sensitive customer records, or access client systems? Which activities apply?
6. Does it use written agreements with clients? Do any agreements require particular insurance limits or wording?
7. Does it engage contractors to deliver client work? What share of the work involves them, and does it request proof of insurance?
8. Have claims or incidents arisen from its services? Give dates and details for reviewer follow-up.
9. What coverage and limits does it want, and when should coverage begin?

## Fictional intake example

- Business: Northstar Systems Studio LLC
- Services: consulting 40%, implementation 25%, custom software 20%, training 10%, hardware resale 5%
- Revenue: $650,000 projected
- Client systems: access is required for implementation
- Sensitive records: customer data may be accessible; record count is unknown
- Contractors: yes; scope and proof of insurance need confirmation
- Client contracts: some specify insurance requirements; documents have not been supplied
- Claims: unknown; ask the applicant directly
- Requested coverage: technology E&O; confirm limits and effective date

The expected review packet should identify the known facts and their source, ask for the record count and contractor details, request contract language where relevant, and keep unanswered claims and coverage questions pending. A reviewer must validate any inferred answer before using it in a real submission.

## Scope and provenance

The questionnaire is an original example informed by general industry concepts. The application support service uses it as its default source document when none is supplied. A user may provide a separate form for their own workflow, but this repository does not distribute a third-party carrier form for that purpose.
